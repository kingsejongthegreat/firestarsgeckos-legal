Privacy Policy — Firestar's Geckos

Last updated: October 2026

Firestar's Geckos is a mobile game. This page explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Almost nothing. Specifically:

  • No account, no sign-in, no personal information. You never give the app your name, email, or any other personal detail.
  • No ads, and no ad-tracking SDKs of any kind. This app will never show ads.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics automatically sends a diagnostic report: what went wrong (stack trace), your device model, OS version, and the app's version number. This does not include your name, email, or anything else that identifies you personally.
  • Purchases, if you choose to buy anything. In-app purchases (optional) are processed by Google Play's billing system and RevenueCat, a payment-processing service. We don't see or store your payment details ourselves — Google and RevenueCat handle that, under their own privacy policies.

What stays on your device

Your save data — your geckos, your collection, your progress — is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere, and the game works fully offline.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone, because we don't collect anything to share in the first place beyond the anonymous crash/purchase data described above.

Children's privacy

Firestar's Geckos is designed to be safe for players of all ages, including children: no ads, no account creation, no chat features, and no personal data collection.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email listed on this app's Google Play Store page.
